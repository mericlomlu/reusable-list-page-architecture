# Metadata review

Verify:

- default title, title template, description, and unique route metadata;
- canonical behavior and metadata base after a production domain is verified;
- favicon, SVG/PNG icons, Apple touch icon, manifest, and theme colors;
- 1200x630 OG image and Twitter card readability;
- robots and sitemap coverage;
- `WebSite` and `SoftwareSourceCode` structured data with verified facts only;
- original assets without framework or company logos.

Report PASS, FAIL, or BLOCKED with a concrete fix. Do not invent URLs or facts.

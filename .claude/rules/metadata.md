# Metadata and public assets

- Use the Next.js Metadata API and file-based metadata conventions.
- Provide unique titles and descriptions for every public route with one title template.
- Include canonical metadata, Open Graph, Twitter card, robots, sitemap, manifest, favicon, Apple touch icon, and theme colors.
- The OG asset is 1200x630 and remains legible at small preview sizes.
- Use verified project and author facts only. Do not invent a production domain before it is supplied.
- Structured data may use `WebSite` and `SoftwareSourceCode` with verified values.
- Favicon and social assets must be original and must not use framework or company logos.
